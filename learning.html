Teaching & design

Curriculum & learning

Lessons, materials, and curriculum shaped by what students understand and what they need to learn next.

I’ve designed original grammar and writing materials, adapted ELA curriculum, and built project-based World History instruction. As a curriculum director, I also worked on the different problem of helping a whole school choose and implement materials across grade levels.

Start with what students are doing

At the classroom level, I’ve used assessment patterns to reconsider what a lesson needs to explain. In ninth-grade grammar, that included teaching the differences between students’ home-language conventions and the conventions expected on standardized tests. I describe that experience in the assessment and instruction story.

Make something worth working on

My project-based work includes World History curriculum and a school STEM lab with 3D printing, carpentry, robotics, and chemistry. I’m interested in tasks that give students a reason to use the knowledge, examine the result, and try again.
