NextTask

A task-prioritization and focus-timer app for choosing what to work on, then getting to work.

A list can tell you everything you need to do and still leave you unsure where to begin. NextTask gives you a way to compare tasks, sort what matters, and choose work for a session. The focus timer tracks time as you work.

I’m also developing the way it handles projects and subtasks. That introduces some less visible questions: which work belongs to which project, how should time add up, and what should happen when a task changes or a session resumes?

The work underneath the interface

Those questions connect to my experience with student data. A record needs to keep its context, and a calculation needs clear definitions. I’ve written about that connection, with images of actual NextTask code, on the data-work page.

NextTask is ongoing work, developed with AI assistance. The public repository includes the implementation and the work of checking and improving its behavior.
