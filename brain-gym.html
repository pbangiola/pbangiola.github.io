The Brain Gym

A gym for your brain: a place to learn and build with tools that are impractical to keep at home.

The comparison is fairly literal. People join a gym because treadmills, weight racks, and other equipment are expensive and take up space. The Brain Gym applies that idea to woodworking tools, electronics, and 3D printing, with instruction and supported projects alongside time to use the equipment.

I’m developing the idea in Oak Park, drawing on my own work as a teacher and maker. The early project ideas include cutting boards, boxes, coat racks, and picture frames: things that give someone a reason to measure, plan, make a cut, and see how the pieces fit together.

From my workshop to a place to learn

The prototype site follows the development of the idea from my own workshop. It also lays out possible offerings and commerce previews. This is a concept in development; the website isn’t taking transactions.
