An introduction

About me

I’m an educator, a father, and someone who likes to understand how things work by building them.

I’ve taught elementary, middle, high school, and university students. I’ve also worked for education technology companies and led curriculum and technology inside a school. Those jobs have given me different views of the same practical question: what would make it easier for someone to learn, or to do the work they’re trying to do?

Sometimes that has meant designing a lesson. Sometimes it has meant figuring out why accounts weren’t working, organizing information, or helping a teacher make sense of assessment results. I like the work of following a problem through those different layers.

What I’m building

The projects on this site give me ways to keep working on those questions. The Brain Gym grows out of my interest in making and hands-on learning. NextTask is about choosing and organizing work. Paddlers Map comes from kayaking and wanting better information about waterways.

The data-work page connects some of that current work to earlier problems I’ve handled in schools and edtech. My detailed résumé gives the professional background.

Education and home

I studied Politics at Princeton and Technology, Innovation and Education at Harvard’s Graduate School of Education. I live in Oak Park, Illinois. Away from the computer and classroom, I’m a kayaker, carpenter, and cook.
