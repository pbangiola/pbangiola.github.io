Experience & background

Over 15 years in education, curriculum leadership, and educational technology — connecting hands-on teaching with better systems for learning.

What I do

Curriculum design · Instructional leadership · Educational technology · Data-informed improvement · Teacher development · Project-based learning

Selected experience

  • Johnson College Prep, Noble Schools (2025–2026): Taught ninth-grade English, designing targeted instruction around literacy assessment and student needs.
  • St. Viator Elementary School (2022–2025): Led K–8 curriculum, instructional improvement, interventions, teacher mentoring, and technology initiatives. During this period, schoolwide math performance rose from the 52nd to 68th percentile and reading from the 61st to 70th.
  • American Prison Data Systems (2014–2017): Supported adoption of secure digital learning tools across correctional facilities, training educators and coordinating with institutional and product teams.
  • Other teaching: Middle-school instruction in Chicago and university-level English instruction in Beijing.
