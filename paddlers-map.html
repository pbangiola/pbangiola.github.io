Paddlers Map

A map that brings rivers, water conditions, and canoe launches together for paddlers.

I’ve been paddling for twenty-five years, and being on the water is where I feel most at home. But in the Chicago suburbs where I live, water levels fluctuate dramatically. That makes it hard to know what I’m getting before I get there.

I built a tool to survey river flow, and now I’m playing with taking it national.

The map combines river gauges, waterways, and launch locations to help paddlers get a sense of conditions before heading out. It’s still a work in progress.
