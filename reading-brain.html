The connection that changes everything

Old circuits.
A learned partnership.

“Hotwiring” is a metaphor for plasticity: literacy tunes parts of the visual system for print and links them with spoken-language networks. It does not splice wires, bypass normal learning, or replace our ability to recognize objects.

For alphabetic reading, instruction makes relationships between letters and speech sounds explicit; practice builds efficient word recognition. Vocabulary and knowledge still do the work of comprehension. A brain map explains a mechanism—it cannot by itself establish which classroom program works best.
